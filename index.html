3SPORTS • three, almost on top of each other

The sports do not separate. That is the result.

Colour the same dots by sport and the three centroids sit within 0.11 of each other across the map's width, while a single sport's own spread averages 0.24. They overlap far more than they separate — and that is the model working as designed. Stage 2 trains a gradient-reversal head whose job is to make the sport unguessable from the embedding. A classifier manages 0.632 against a 0.626 majority baseline: six thousandths better than always answering "basketball".

Centroids are computed in your browser from the dots on screen.

Data provenance. Every dot and every tile on this page is read at runtime from assets/unified.json, the output of this repo's own pipeline/export_unified_stage2.py: each player-season encoded through the Stage 2.1 checkpoint's drifted per-sport encoders and shared trunk to a 64-d L2-normalised vector, then PCA-3 of that space. 20,721 player-seasons — 12,966 basketball, 5,325 American football, 2,430 football. Two derived files are committed alongside it, public/assets/unified_map.json and public/assets/unified_roster.json, each rebuildable with scripts/build_unified_map.py. The only transform applied here is a view transform: PC1 and PC2 are rescaled from their observed range into the canvas box and y is flipped so positive PC2 renders upward; PC3 exists in the export and is dropped. No value on this page is generated, seeded, sampled or interpolated. What the gates say. Sport-invariance (G2) is the headline and it is weak: a sport classifier on the joint space scores 0.632 against a 0.626 majority baseline, a margin of 0.0063. The export's own note is worth quoting — "met" there means within ten points of the achievable floor, so read the margin, not the label. Not shown, deliberately. The export carries a cross_arch field of k-means-style cluster ids (A0, A1, A2, A3, A5, A11) and earlier versions of this page rendered them as named archetypes — HOOPS_GUARD, GRIDIRON_QB, EQUITY_MOM, SCHOOL_W. Those names were never derived from cluster membership, and two of them named domains this model does not contain: there is no equities tower and no schools tower here, only hoops, gridiron and pitch. The news tower is real code reading a real file, and that file currently holds zero entities.
