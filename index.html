What this page will not tell you

Read this before quoting any number above

  • A classifier can still tell which sport a row came from — accuracy against a majority-class baseline, a margin of just . The gate records this as met, but the artifact defines that as “within 10 points of the achievable floor” and says in the same breath that this is a weak bar — quote the margin, not the status. So the margin is what this page quotes. The larger “vs chance” figure is not used anywhere here: with classes at 62.6/25.7/11.7, chance is the wrong reference.
  • Gate 4 is close to circular. Cross-sport neighbours share an archetype 98% of the time, but the archetype map is hand-authored and the contrastive loss was trained on exactly those labels. Drop that loss and the number falls to 0.204 against a 0.171 random baseline — i.e. chance. Gate 4 measures that training converged, not that anything was discovered.
  • These are analogies, not equivalences. The nearest cross-sport neighbour of a player-season is the model’s best guess inside a space built to pull same-archetype rows together. It is not evidence that two athletes are alike.
  • One gate-1 sub-metric is history, not telemetry. The shipped checkpoint’s position-drop value was 0.0 for every sport by construction — a mask was used as an index, so both arms scored 1.0 and their difference could only be zero. Displayed values come from a corrected probe; the checkpoint field is stale until a retrain.
  • Seasons are not evenly covered. 30 era buckets span 1996–2025, but the three sports contribute very different volumes — see the counts in the header before reading anything into a sparse region of the map.
